Chicken Cordon Bleu

Description

I used to think that these were impossible to make.
After challenging myself to make it, I discovered that they were not super complex, but they do tend to make one hell of a mess in a small kitchen.
If you prepare your ingredients in advance and give yourself plenty of time to clean as you go it is not that bad.

ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 8 slices of ham or prosciutto
  • 2 large beaten eggs
  • 4 ounces of cheese (Swiss, Gruyere, Munster, Smoked Gouda or whatever melty cheese you like) cut into 1/2 inch thick sticks
  • 1 cup of Flour (or rice flour for gluten free)
  • 2 cups of bread crumbs (whatever kind you like, use crushed pork rinds for a gluten free version)
  • 2 Tablespoons of Dijon Mustard
  • 1 cup of Oil (Vegetable Oil, Avacado Oil, Lard, Tallow whatever you got with a high smoke-point)
  • Kosher Salt, Fresh Ground Black Pepper, Garlic Powder and Ground Tyme
  • 1/2 a cup of chopped Fresh Parsley
  • Lemon Wedges for garnish

Prepare the Chicken:

This takes a bit of time to prepare. You can bread and cook these immediately, but what I like to do to make it "next level" is prepare the stuffed breasts first. Then let them 'air brine' uncovered on a wire rack in the fridge overnight to let the flavors develop and cook them the next day. Depending on how much time you have either way works but the air brine brings a lot more flavor to the final product.

  1. Butterfly cut your chicken breasts and place them on a sheet of parchment paper.
  2. Cover the breasts with another sheet of parchment paper and lightly tenderize them until they are about a uniform quarter inch thick
  3. Lightly sprinkle one side with salt, pepper, garlic powder and tyme, then spread a thin layer of dijon mustard over the top and sprinkle with fresh parsley.
  4. Layer your ham or prosciutto over the top and place a stick of your cheese on the left side.
  5. Starting from the cheese side, roll the chicken over the cheese and try to tuck the ends in to make a nice package. Use toothpicks or skewers to pin it shut.
  6. Once all 4 are done you can either move on to the next step of breading and cooking, or sprinkle lightly with salt and pepper and air brine overnight.

Breading and Cooking:

  1. Start off by pre-heating the oven to 300o Farenheit. Then get your oil in a frying pan preheated to around 350o
  2. Set up 3 pans or dishes in a row on the counter next to your hot oil
  3. In the first pan mix your flour with salt, pepper, garlic powder and tyme
  4. In the second pan beat your eggs silly with just a tiny splash of water or milk
  5. In the third pan spread out your breadcrumbs or breadcrumb alternatives. (For a darker crust mix in 1/4 cup of grated parmesean cheese,[optional])
  6. When your oil comes to temperature roll and completely coat your chicken from pan 1 to 2 to 3 making sure everything sticks well. Feel free to press the crumbs in by hand to make a nice coating.
  7. Place your first piece of chicken in the hot oil and let it cook for 3 minutes per side while you prepare the next piece.
  8. When your first piece is just beginning to turn light golden brown, remove it from the oil and place on a paper towel lined plate and start frying the next piece.
  9. When all 4 pieces are finished place them in an oven safe dish or pan and place in the pre-heated oven for 20-30 minutes until your food thermometer reads an internal temperature of 160o Farenheit.
  10. Remove from oven and let rest for 5 minutes, then plate with a wedge of lemon and a garnish of fresh parsley.

    11. This recipe is initially challenging but it gets easier every time I make it. Feel free to play around with the seasonings, spices, breadcrumb substitutions and cheese varieties until you find your signature flavor.
